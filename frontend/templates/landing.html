Built for clarity. Designed with humility.

Risk management over return maximization

Capital preservation comes first. We surface drawdowns, concentration, and tail risk before chasing upside.

Long-term robustness over short-term moves

We design for decade-long compounding, not the next tick. Patience is a strategy, and we encode it.
