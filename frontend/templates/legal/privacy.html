Privacy policy

Version 0.1 · last updated 12 October 2026

Agentfolio connects to your broker account to show your portfolio, explain it with AI, and, if you choose, propose trades that only you can approve. This page explains what we collect, why, who else sees it, and how to delete it.

What we store

  • Your WhatsApp number: how you sign in, and where daily briefs are sent.
  • Your broker credentials (Angel One API key, client ID, PIN and TOTP secret): encrypted at rest, and decrypted only in server memory to talk to your broker.
  • Your app password: stored only as a one-way hash. Nobody, including us, can read it back.
  • What you set up: your risk profile, your daily-brief schedule and its delivery log, and, on Premium, your saved conversations with the AI agents.
  • Trade decisions: when you approve or reject a proposed trade, a record of the order, the checks it went through and the outcome.

Your holdings, positions, orders and funds are read from your broker when you open the app. We keep them only in short-lived memory caches, not in our database.

What stays on your phone

The app keeps your sign-in session cookie in the phone's secure storage. If you add your own OpenRouter key for AI insights, it is kept only in the phone's secure storage and is sent to our server with each request, which passes it to OpenRouter and does not store it. Signing out removes both.

Who else processes your data

  • Your broker (Angel One), to read your portfolio and place orders you approve.
  • OpenRouter and the AI model it routes to, which receive your portfolio summary and your questions to write insights and agent replies.
  • Our WhatsApp delivery service, which receives your number and the text of your daily brief.
  • Google News and Yahoo Finance, which receive sector names and stock symbols, never anything that identifies you.
  • Firebase Crashlytics (Google), which receives crash reports and app usage events: screens viewed, buttons tapped, request timings, app and device model, and a one-way hash of your account id. Never amounts, credentials, phone numbers or chat text. You can turn usage events off in the app under Account → Privacy.
  • Our hosting and database providers, which store the data above on our behalf.

We do not sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps.

How long we keep it

Until you delete your account. Deleting removes everything listed under "What we store" except trade decision records, which we keep, no longer linked to you, as an audit trail of decisions made on the platform.

Deleting your account

In the app: Account → Delete account. Or on the web, without the app: agentfolio.in/delete-account. Deleting does not close or change your broker account.
