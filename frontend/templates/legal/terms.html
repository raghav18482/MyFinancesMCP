What Agentfolio is

Agentfolio is a tool for looking at your own broker portfolio, with AI-written explanations and, on Premium, an AI agent that can propose trades. It is not a broker, an investment adviser or a research analyst, and nothing it shows is a recommendation to buy or sell.

Your account

You connect a broker account that is yours, with credentials you are entitled to use. Keep your app password private: anyone with it and your WhatsApp number can see your portfolio and approve trades. You can delete your account at any time.

Trades

The agent only proposes. An order is placed when you approve it, through your broker, on your broker's terms. Approved orders cannot be cancelled or reversed through Agentfolio. You are responsible for every order you approve. Read the risk disclosure.

AI output

AI insights, summaries, predictions and agent replies can be wrong, out of date or incomplete. Check anything important against your broker and your own judgement.

Availability

We work to keep the service running but cannot promise it always will. Market data, news and broker connections come from third parties and can fail or be delayed.

Liability

To the extent the law allows, Agentfolio is not liable for trading losses or for decisions made using the app.
