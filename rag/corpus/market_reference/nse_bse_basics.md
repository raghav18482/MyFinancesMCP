# India equity market basics

## Exchanges

**NSE** (National Stock Exchange) and **BSE** (Bombay Stock Exchange) are the primary cash equity exchanges in India. Cash equity symbols often end with **-EQ** on broker APIs.

## Order types (generic)

Common order varieties include **LIMIT** (price capped), **MARKET** (execute at best available), and **SL** / **SL-M** (stop-loss variants). Exact names depend on your broker and product (CNC vs MIS vs NRML).

## Circuit limits

Stocks can hit **upper** or **lower** circuit filters when price moves beyond exchange-defined daily bands; trading may halt or only allow one side until the next session or auction rules apply.

## Indices

**NIFTY 50** tracks large-cap Indian equities; **SENSEX** is a BSE index of 30 large stocks. These are used as benchmarks for portfolio comparison in analytics features.
